ARDUINO WORKSHOP(BASIC)

Date: 10th October 2025

The IEEE EDS IEM Student Branch successfully organized an engaging Arduino Workshop aimed at introducing participants to the fundamentals of embedded systems and microcontroller-based projects. The session witnessed an enthusiastic participation of 30 students, all eager to explore the world of Arduino and its practical applications in electronics and innovation.
The session was led by two of our talented core committee members:
• Ms. Sristi Seal, Content Lead
• Ms. Ishani Ray Chaudhury, Social Media Manager

SEASSION 1: INTRODUCTION TO ARDUINO
In the first half of the workshop, Ms. Sristi Seal provided an excellent introduction to the Arduino Uno board. She explained the key components, their functions, and real-world importance.
Participants were introduced to the Arduino IDE, where they learned how to run built-in example codes and operate the on-board LED. Her clear explanations and hands-on demonstrations made it easy for beginners to grasp the core concepts of Arduino programming and circuit setup.

Session 2: CODE UPGRADATION & PROJECT INTEGRATION
The second session, conducted by Ms. Ishani Ray Chaudhury, focused on advancing the knowledge gained in the first half. She demonstrated how to modify and upgrade Arduino codes, integrate external devices, and achieve different outputs. Participants learned how varying code logic and connected components can lead to the development of diverse Arduino-based projects. This session inspired participants to think creatively and explore new possibilities in embedded systems and automation.

Special Demonstration: GESTURE-CONTROLLED CAR PROJECT
Adding an exciting highlight to the workshop, one of the participants showcased their advanced Arduino project — a Hand Gesture Controlled Car.
The live demonstration captured everyone's attention, offering a glimpse into the potential of Arduino in robotics and IoT applications. The innovative project and its real-time control system left the audience highly motivated and curious to build their own projects.

ML WORKSHOP

Date: 10th-11th September 2025

The Machine Learning Workshop was conducted on 10th and 11th September 2025, by the IEEE EDS Student Branch Chapter, providing a very hands-on learning experience for participants over the course of two sessions conducted by Mr. Rohit Majhi.

DAY 1: INTRODUCTION TO MACHINE LEARNING
On the first day of the workshop, we started off the 90-minute session that set the tone for everything that followed. We explored the origins of Machine Learning, who invented it and how it has evolved over the course of 65 years to be where it is now. The discussion then unfolded into the fascinating world of machine learning, helping us understand why it has become such a driving force in modern technology. Towards the end of the session, the endless possibilities that arise when Machine Learning and Artificial Intelligence meets the Internet of Things (IoT), opened up the floor for discussion of the various questions the participants possessed.

DAY 2: HANDS-ON MACHINE LEARNING
On the second day of the workshop, the excitement carried forward into a hands-on 90-minute session where we built our very own machine learning model using Python and its various Machine Learning libraries in Google Colab. It was an incredibly interactive and eye-opening experience, as we moved from theory to practical—seeing our code come to life through a supervised learning project. The session left everyone inspired and more confident, offering a real sense of what it means to create something intelligent from data.

PROJECT MAKING
After the learning phase of the workshop concluded, participants were given the exciting opportunity to put their knowledge into action by creating their own Unsupervised Machine Learning projects on selected topics. This was conducted via online mode. It was truly inspiring to see their creativity and understanding come alive through the models they built. For their hard work and innovation, the most outstanding projects were honored with Certificates of Excellence, marking a proud and fulfilling end to the workshop.

FET 100

Date: 29th August 2025

The IEEE Electron Devices Society (EDS) Student Chapter of the Institute of Engineering and Management (IEM) successfully organized a Distinguished Lecture titled “FET 100 - Celebrating 100 Years of Field Effect Transistor (FET)” on 29th August 2025 at 4:00 PM in the IEM Gurukul Building. The session was graced by Prof. (Dr.) Manoj Saxena, Chair - IEEE EDS Delhi Chapter (India), a distinguished academician and researcher in the field of semiconductor devices. The event was conducted under the guidance of Prof. Dr. Rajib Ghosh, Faculty Coordinator, IEEE EDS IEM Student Chapter.
This landmark session was organized to commemorate 100 years of the Field Effect Transistor (FET) — one of the most transformative inventions in electronics. The lecture aimed to provide a comprehensive understanding of the transistor's historical journey, working principles, and future role in emerging technologies.

The event commenced with a warm welcome address by the faculty coordinator, setting the tone for an enriching session. Following this, Prof. (Dr.) Manoj Saxena delivered his expert talk, taking the audience through the evolution of the Field Effect Transistor — from its conceptual origin to its modern-day applications in microelectronics and integrated circuits.
He elaborated on the fundamental working principles of FETs, their various types, and technological advancements that have shaped the semiconductor industry over the decades. The lecture also covered the role of FETs in VLSI design, nanoelectronics, and device fabrication, emphasizing their importance in shaping modern computing and communication systems.
Prof. Saxena also discussed emerging semiconductor technologies such as FinFETs, MOSFET scaling challenges, and the future scope of FETs in sustainable and high-performance electronics. His talk provided students with a blend of theoretical depth and industrial relevance, encouraging them to explore research and innovation in the field of electronic device design.
The session saw the active participation of 104 students from various departments, including Electronics and Communication Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Computer Science Engineering, and Information Technology. The audience engaged enthusiastically during the interactive Q&A session, where Prof. Saxena addressed thought-provoking questions on transistor miniaturization, fabrication techniques, and real-world challenges in semiconductor research.
The event concluded with a vote of thanks and a memento presentation to the esteemed speaker as a token of appreciation. The session ended with a group photograph, capturing the success and collaborative spirit of the occasion.

LENSCAPE

Date: 22nd-23rd August 2025

The IEEE EDS IEM Student Branch successfully organized a two-day photography workshop titled “Lenscape”, designed to provide students with a creative platform to learn, explore, and practice the art of photography. The event witnessed enthusiastic participation from 32 students, all eager to enhance their photography skills under the guidance of professional photographers.

Day 1: INTRODUCTION TO PHOTOGRAPHY AND CrREATIVE INSIGHTS
The first day of Lenscape was conducted by Mr. Mihir Lal Saha and renowned photographer Mr. Amitabh Mukherjee, both of whom shared their extensive experience and artistic perspectives on photography.
During this one-hour interactive session, the mentors showcased a collection of their personally captured photographs, using them to explain the fundamentals of photography, including lighting, composition, framing, and exposure.
Participants were also introduced to different genres of photography, such as street, portrait, architectural, and nature photography.
By the end of Day 1, participants were invited to submit their best photographs, based on which the top 10 photographers were shortlisted for an exclusive photo walk on Day 2.

Day 2: NORTH KOLKATA PHOTO WALK
The second day of the workshop was led by Mr. Mihir Lal Saha and renowned photographer Mr. Deb Lahiri. The top 10-12 shortlisted participants embarked on a photo walk through the heritage lanes of North Kolkata, guided by their mentors.
Throughout the walk, Mr. Deb Lahiri provided live guidance and professional tips, helping participants understand how to capture the essence of the city, its old architecture, and its vibrant street life. The participants explored the vintage charm of North Kolkata, experimenting with lighting, perspective, and storytelling through their lenses.
This immersive experience not only enhanced their technical skills but also encouraged them to view photography as a form of creative expression.
